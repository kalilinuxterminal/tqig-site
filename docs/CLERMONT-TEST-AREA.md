# Clermont sample journeys and future testing

At the user's request, future device experiments and the website's default sample journeys use Clermont, Florida. The world map, landing preview and first quick destination also start in Clermont. This does not restrict worldwide search or route planning.

The sample origin is Clermont City Hall (685 W. Montrose Street), and the destination is Waterfront Park. The city identifies the park at 100 3rd Street, along Lake Minneola between East Avenue and Eighth Street. Public landmark references: [City Hall](https://www.clermontfl.gov/161/City-Hall), [Waterfront Park](https://www.clermontfl.gov/Facilities/Facility/Details/Waterfront-Park-30). Sample coordinates were resolved from Photon/OpenStreetMap; the routing provider snaps them to its walking or driving network.

These are public sample targets, not the user's actual current position or a customer's home. Previously observed New York evidence remains historical evidence rather than being relabelled as Clermont. New algorithm fixtures use synthetic Clermont-area geometry and do not establish actual road conditions.

Local road geometry and optional signal locations come from the selected routing/OpenStreetMap providers. Planned light stops do not represent live Clermont signal phases. Traffic-light timing, road closures, background app behavior and third-party driving telemetry are not inferred from a sample route.
